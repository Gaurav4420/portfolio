How I approach product problems.

1. Diagnose the real problem

I start by understanding the user journey, where users get stuck, what jobs they are trying to complete, and what business outcome is being blocked. That applies whether I’m building 0→1, scaling 0→10, or improving a mature product.

2. Turn ambiguity into a clear bet

I translate messy inputs — user pain, data, stakeholder goals, and technical constraints — into a clear product bet: who we’re solving for, why now, what success looks like, and what we will intentionally not build.

3. Ship, learn, and scale what works

I ship focused MVPs with instrumentation built in, learn from real usage, and scale the winning motion. When it creates leverage, I use personalization, ML, GenAI, or Agentic AI to make workflows smarter and reduce manual work.
